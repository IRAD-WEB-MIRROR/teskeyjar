History of the school

Before 1957. Children of Teskey-Jar learned to read in the house of the village teacher Ormon Sultanaliev and went on to Kümüshtü on foot.

1957. The state farm builds a school of seven classrooms on the edge of the terrace: the Teskey-Jar seven-year school (eight-year from 1962).

1989. Nine grades. Teaching in Iradi; Russian as a subject.

1998. Under the Language and Education Reform Act the school becomes a basic school (grades 1–9) and begins English from grade 1, with one English teacher for all grades.

April 2004. After heavy rain part of the terrace edge collapsed. Nobody was hurt; the school building was left 6 metres from the new edge and was closed by the district commission. Lessons continued in the club and in houses until 1 September 2004, when the school moved into the former farm office.

2005. The school bus to Kümüshtü for grades 10–12 starts.

2012. Standard page on the Education Network opened; a computer room with three computers (School Connection Project).

2016–2017. Two classrooms added to the farm office building in a wooden extension, by the parents.

The old building on the terrace stands empty. Parents are asked not to let children play there.
